# The adjuster queue is not the bottleneck

**Author:** Jonas Berg

**Date:** 2026-07-14

**Kind:** Observation

We have been treating adjuster capacity as the reason claims take four days.
The cycle-time breakdown for June says otherwise.

Of the median 4.1 days from first notice to offer, assignment accounts for
about three hours. The rest is waiting: 2.6 days of it is the gap between
the claim being opened and the insured sending usable damage photos.

| Stage                      | Median  |
| -------------------------- | ------- |
| FNOL to claim opened       | 4m      |
| Claim opened to photos in  | 2.6d    |
| Photos in to assignment    | 3h      |
| Assignment to offer        | 1.4d    |

Two things follow. First, hiring adjusters would move the number very little.
Second, the straight-through work is aimed at the right place only if it also
shortens the photo gap — settling faster after the photos arrive still leaves
two and a half days on the table.

Worth asking why the gap is that long. The prompt to send photos goes out in
the confirmation email, which is also the email with the claim number, which
is the email people save and do not read twice.
