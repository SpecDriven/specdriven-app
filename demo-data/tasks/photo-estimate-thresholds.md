# Agree the photo-estimate severity threshold

**Assigned:** Priya Raman

**Estimate:** 1h 30m

Straight-through claims settle without an adjuster only under the conditions
in the roadmap's straight-through test, and the severity line — currently
$3,500 — is the one nobody has signed off.

Claims and Actuarial read it differently: Claims wants the ceiling high
enough that the glass and low-severity comprehensive work actually leaves the
queue, Actuarial wants it low enough that the first year's leakage is
measurable against a control group.

## What this task is

Bring one number to the table with the evidence behind it, not three options.

- Pull last year's closed collision and comprehensive claims by severity band.
- For each band: how many would have passed the other four conditions, and
  what an adjuster actually paid versus the photo estimate.
- Recommend one threshold, and say what we expect it to cost in leakage.

## Done when

Claims and Actuarial have both agreed the number in writing, and it is
written into the straight-through test on the roadmap page.
