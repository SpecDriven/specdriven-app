# Rewrite the lapsed-policy denial letter

**Assigned:** Aisha Bello

**Estimate:** 45m

The "Accident on a lapsed policy" scenario
(claims/auto-accidents/record-car-accident.feature.md) denies the claim and
sends a letter saying the policy was not in force on the date of loss. The
scenario is right; the letter is not.

Compliance flagged the current wording in the last review: it states the
outcome and nothing else. Someone who has just had an accident and believed
they were covered gets a paragraph that reads as though they did something
wrong, with no date, no reason for the cancellation, and no reinstatement
path.

## What this task is

Rewrite the letter body. The behaviour does not change — this is wording.

- Name the cancellation date and the reason on record.
- Name the date of loss, so the gap is visible rather than asserted.
- Say plainly what the options are, including reinstatement where the term
  allows it.
- Keep it under a page, and out of the passive voice.

## Done when

Compliance has approved the draft and the template is in the claims
correspondence set.
