# Draft the telematics consent screen

**Assigned:** Dana Whitfield

**Estimate:** 1h

Usage-based auto needs the driver's consent before the app collects a single
trip, and consent collected badly is worse than no programme at all — it is
the part of this that ends up in a regulator's letter.

The roadmap page commits us to pricing on how people drive. It does not yet
say what we tell them we are collecting, how long we keep it, or what happens
to their premium if they stop sharing.

## What this task is

Draft the screen and the three answers it has to give, in the customer's
words rather than the policy's.

- What is collected: which signals, at what frequency, and what is not
  collected.
- How long it is kept, and what happens to it if they leave.
- What withdrawing consent does to their premium — including whether it
  reverts or re-rates at renewal.

## Done when

Legal and Marketing have both signed off the copy, and the retention period
matches what the data platform is actually configured to do.
